Enterprise Security Posture

Security by architectural intent.

THE KOLLI considers operational security throughout product architecture, continuous deployment pipelines, and database modeling. Zero trust, full encryption, and immutable audit controls.

01 / Data Protection

Encryption at rest and in transit

All sensitive database volumes use AES-256 encryption at rest. Internal and external communication channels strictly enforce TLS 1.3 with automated certificate rotation.

02 / Access Governance

Principle of least privilege (PoLP)

Access to infrastructure and production workloads is governed by hardware MFA, short-lived ephemeral tokens, and granular role-based access control (RBAC).

03 / Privacy by Design

Zero telemetry & data sovereignty

Proprietary software platforms (KLERP, KLAB, KLAI, KLORA) isolate client data. We never sell, monetize, or train general AI models on customer information.

04 / Continuous Auditing

Vulnerability scanning & updates

Automated software dependency vulnerability scanners (SAST & DAST) operate continuously across our CI/CD deployment pipelines with zero-day remediation protocols.
